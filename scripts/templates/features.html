THE AXIOM FEATURE GUIDE

From the service plan
to the presentation screen.

Prepare your content, arrange the flow and control what goes live. Here’s what Axiom can do today—and what’s still being developed.

Available now · Axiom Pro

Your whole service,
clearly organized.

Create services and presentation sections. Add lyrics or text, reorder slides, duplicate a presentation and reuse songs from Playlists.

  • Thumbnail and list views
  • Editable text, fonts, colours and positioning
  • Independent copies when reusing content
Available now · Axiom Pro

Bring your media.
Keep your workflow.

Present local images and supported video files. Import PDF and PPTX pages into your service, alongside lyrics and Bible passages.

  • PDF and PowerPoint pages import as still image slides
  • Video playback and separate audience output
  • Local desktop imports use your computer’s storage

PowerPoint animations, original editable shapes and embedded media are not preserved. Shared cloud uploads currently have a 100 MB per-file limit.

Available now · Axiom Pro

Preview first.
Present when ready.

See what comes next without changing the audience’s screen. Go Live sends your prepared slide to Program. Prefer a direct workflow? Select Program view for one-click presentation.

  • Preview, Program and service timer
  • Blackout and clear-text controls
  • Adjustable pane widths and Charcoal/Pearl themes
Available now · Offline Scripture

Read the passage.
See the reference.

Search by reference, chapter or words from a verse, then add selected verses to your service. New Bible slides have a dedicated reading layout with a distinct reference and translation label.

  • Standalone: WEB offline, KJV online or authorized import
  • Church workspace: WEB British, ASV, BSB, MSB, BBE, Darby and Young’s Literal
  • Eight complete offline English editions in connected mode

Full bundled KJV distribution remains pending UK permission. NIV, NLT, ESV and other copyrighted editions are not included without licences.

Available now · Connected church workspace

One agenda.
A shared conversation.

The program team prepares service agendas in AxiomLeader and sends them to Tech. Pro receives the service, messages and files, with the context attached to the right gathering.

  • Service-linked agendas, notes, messages and files
  • Tech retains prepared slides and private operator notes
  • Role-based invitations and service handoffs
Available now · AxiomU

Carry the message
into the week.

Review and publish the summaries, Scripture and updates you want members to see. Members revisit published content and keep personal notes.

  • Explicit preview and publish actions
  • Private leadership notes excluded from member publishing
  • Mobile-friendly reading and personal reflection
Available in beta

Live Bible assistance

Connected Pro can listen for verse references and a starter set of familiar passages. This is limited phrase matching in English. Speech support varies by browser, and Tech reviews a suggestion before presenting.
