Ready for the service

Add content, choose a page and press Go Live. Preparing another page does not change the live screen.

Live screen: allow popups, use extended displays, move the output window onto the projector, then press F for fullscreen. Esc exits fullscreen.

Shortcuts: Left/Right advances live pages, B toggles blackout, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redoes.

Imports: PDF pages and .pptx slides become images. PowerPoint animations, embedded videos, charts and SmartArt may not carry across. For the closest visual match, export PowerPoint as PDF first. Legacy .ppt files need conversion to .pptx or PDF.

Bibles: import JSON shaped as {"translation":"Name","verses":[{"book":"John","chapter":3,"verse":16,"text":"…"}]}. Only import a translation you have permission to use.

Backups: export includes web media. Saving is local to this browser; connect your church to share messages and files. Presentation edits stay on this device.

Video audio: playback may require a click on the output window. Use MP4 with H.264/AAC for broad compatibility.
