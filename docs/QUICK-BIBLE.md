# Quick Bible presentation

Add Bible opens passage search directly. Choose an available translation, search a reference or words, then click a result to send that verse straight to Program. Searching by itself does not change the audience output.

Reference examples: `Mat 3 5`, `Matt3:5`, `Jn3 16`, `1Cor13 4-7`, `Psalm 23 1`. Common abbreviations, spaces, colons, periods, verse markers and ranges are normalized. Ambiguous book names are not silently guessed. Edition text and verse numbering are retained; one edition is never substituted for another.

The **Scriptures** tab beside Playlist keeps recent successful passage searches and their text on this device. Click a saved passage to go Live again, including when its text was cached from an online lookup. Previous/Next navigates that passage. Presenting a regular service slide returns navigation to the normal presentation. Quick Bible presentation does not add duplicate items to the middle pane or publish unreviewed text to AxiomU.

The recent list retains up to 100 searches and 2,000 verses, removing the oldest searches when necessary. A cached workspace-only edition cannot be replayed after switching to Standalone. Standalone includes offline WEB and online KJV lookup, plus authorized user imports. Connected workspaces expose their included additional offline editions. No unlicensed commercial editions are added by this feature.
